My Graduation Project

"The Chinese Dream" is a visual feast that transcends time and space, blending 3D animation, live-action footage, and ink-wash painting styles. The film embarks from the ink-washed river of history, traversing through to the fast-paced modern era, showcasing China's iconic achievements one by one. At its climax, a Chinese dragon soars into the sky, symbolizing the nation's ascent and heralding a future of boundless brightness and ever-growing prosperity.

《中国梦宣传短片》融合三维动画、实拍摄影与水墨风，演绎一场跨越时空的视觉盛宴。影片从水墨晕染的历史长河出发，穿越至高速发展的现代，将中国的标志性成就一一呈现。高潮之处，中国龙破空而出，象征着中华腾飞之势，寓意着未来无限光明，越来越好。
